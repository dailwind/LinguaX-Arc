Privacy Policy for LinguaX Arc: Corpus Toolkit
Effective Date: October 11, 2026

Developer / Publisher: Dailwind Studios

App Package Name: com.aistudio.linguaxarc.lhvqkp

1. Introduction
Welcome to LinguaX Arc: Corpus Toolkit. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any data on your device. This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles your information.

2. Data Collection and Usage
LinguaX Arc is a 100% offline, on-device mobile corpus analysis toolkit.

No Personal Data Collection: We do not collect, store, track, or share any personal information, user identity, or device details.

Local Processing: All text analysis, concordance searches, frequency profiling, sentiment scoring, and document conversions (.docx/.pdf to text) are performed entirely locally on your device.

No Cloud Storage: Your corpus files, uploaded texts, and search queries never leave your phone or tablet. We do not upload your data to any remote servers or cloud databases.

3. Third-Party Services
Because LinguaX Arc operates fully offline, it does not integrate with third-party analytics trackers, advertising networks, or external data collection tools.

4. Children’s Privacy
Our app does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13, as no data of any kind is collected from any user.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be updated directly within our public repository and documentation.

6. Contact Us
If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, feel free to reach out via our GitHub repository or developer contact page.
